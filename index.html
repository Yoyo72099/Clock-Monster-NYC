LOST TIME MONSTER

New Yorkers lose time every day — waiting for trains, standing in lines, scrolling. Here, you report the minutes you lost, like “12 minutes, L train delay.”

Instead of disappearing, those minutes are eaten by the city’s broken clocks, which combine into one monster clock. It grows bigger and happier with every minute, until it bursts.

You can also take your time back by writing what you wish you’d done with it. The monster shrinks.

The monsters are drawn after real New York public clocks — you’ll see which one in Dress up.

A day starts with a new baby monster at midnight, New York time.
